# Target Market — India Solar EPC (Priority 1)

## Geography

**Primary:** India

**Priority states:**
- Gujarat
- Maharashtra
- Rajasthan
- Karnataka
- Tamil Nadu
- Delhi NCR

## Company profile

Solar EPC companies, solar integrators, and solar distributors operating in India.

**Ideal size:**
- 10–200 employees, or
- ₹5–50 Cr annual revenue, or
- 50+ active solar projects per year

**Project types:** Residential, commercial, and industrial rooftop or ground-mounted solar installations.

## Decision makers

Target people with authority to adopt business software:

- Founder / Co-founder
- Managing Director / Director
- CEO / COO
- Operations Head / VP Operations
- Sales Head / Business Development Head
- General Manager

Avoid: junior sales executives, interns, HR, IT-only roles without business authority.

## Firmographic signals

Companies that likely need techHind Solar CRM:

- Still using Excel, Google Sheets, or Tally for lead and order tracking
- Using a generic CRM (Zoho, HubSpot) without solar-specific workflows
- Running Meta/Facebook lead ads but managing leads manually
- Growing EPC with separate tools for sales, warehouse, and finance
- Handling B2B dealer/distributor orders alongside direct EPC work
- Struggling with quotation speed, inventory serial tracking, or payment follow-ups

## Industry keywords

Solar EPC, solar integrator, solar installer, renewable energy company, solar distributor, solar project developer, rooftop solar, commercial solar, industrial solar, MNRE, DISCOM.

## Disqualifiers (do not target)

- Pure module/inverter manufacturers (unless they also do EPC)
- Large utility-scale developers with custom enterprise ERP
- IT/software companies, digital agencies, consultancies
- Companies outside India (unless Phase 1 India data is insufficient)
- Residential-only electricians with no solar focus
- Government bodies and DISCOM offices
