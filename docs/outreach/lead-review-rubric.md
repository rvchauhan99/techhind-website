# Lead Review Rubric — Phase 1 (`find 30`)

Use this rubric to score each row in the Phase 1 CSV export before spending any BetterContact email credits.

## Scoring

| Score | Label | Criteria |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **3** | Strong fit | Indian solar EPC/integrator/distributor; decision-maker title (Owner, Director, Ops Head, GM); `reason` column mentions solar, installation, EPC, quotations, inventory, or CRM pain |
| **2** | Possible fit | Energy, construction, or renewable company in India; may do solar; title is plausible decision-maker |
| **1** | Weak fit | Generic company; wrong or vague industry; wrong country; `reason` does not connect to solar CRM needs |
| **0** | Reject | Clearly wrong — IT agency, unrelated industry, outside India, junk profile |

## Pass threshold

**Continue to Phase 2 only if ≥ 30% of leads score 2 or 3.**

For 30 leads: need **9 or more** usable leads (score 2 or 3).

## Decision

- [ ] **PASS** — Fit rate ≥ 30% → proceed to Phase 2 (`find 10 emails` + `send 5`)
- [ ] **FAIL** — Fit rate < 30% → refine `target-india.md` and re-run `find 30` (free)
- [ ] **STOP** — Failed twice → India data likely too thin; consider secondary geography

## Phase 2 email quality check

Before sending, also verify in `phase2-with-emails.csv`:

- [ ] Email domain looks corporate (`@company.co.in`, `@company.com`) — not all personal Gmail
- [ ] Company name matches a real solar/EPC business
- [ ] No duplicate companies in the batch
