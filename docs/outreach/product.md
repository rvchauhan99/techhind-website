# techHind Solar CRM — Product Description (OpenOutreach)

## What we sell

techHind Solar CRM is India's complete Solar Management System for EPC companies, distributors, and integrators. It manages the full business journey — from marketing lead to installed project, payment collection, and after-sales service — including in-house kit assembly and warehouse production, in one platform built for the Indian solar market.

Sales, operations, warehouse, production, finance, and service teams work from one system with role-based workspaces, approval workflows, and professional document generation — from quotation PDFs to B2B invoices, picklists, and warranty cards.

## Who benefits

| Team | What they gain |
|------|----------------|
| Sales & marketing | Lead pipeline, inquiries, quotations, conversion tracking |
| Operations | Order execution, fabrication, installation, delivery |
| Warehouse | Purchase orders, stock, serial tracking, dispatch |
| Production | BOM recipes, work orders, kit assembly booking, finished goods |
| Finance | Collections, outstanding tracking, B2B invoicing |
| Service | Tickets, warranty, spare parts |
| Management | Dashboards, KPIs, approvals, reports |

## Key differentiators

- **Complete lifecycle in one system** — No gaps between sales, operations, and finance
- **Built for Indian solar** — DISCOM workflows, subsidy docs, panel/inverter serial tracking, installation sign-off
- **Meta Lead Ads integration** — Facebook and Instagram leads sync automatically into the CRM
- **GST-ready quotations** — Branded PDF proposals with manager approval workflow
- **B2B trading** — Dealer quotes, orders, shipments, invoices, payment tracking
- **Serialized inventory** — Panel and inverter serial capture through installation
- **Production / Assembly** — Versioned BOMs, work orders, picklists, and atomic kit booking into finished-good stock
- **Commission management** — Partner incentives with approval and payout tracking
- **Field-ready** — Site teams capture surveys, photos, and updates from the field

## Pain points we solve

- Leads scattered across Excel, WhatsApp, and Meta ads with no single pipeline
- Slow quotation turnaround and manual GST PDF creation
- No visibility from order confirmation through fabrication and installation
- Inventory and serial numbers tracked in spreadsheets
- Kit assembly and finished-good stock disconnected from warehouse and sales
- Payment outstanding and follow-ups managed manually
- B2B dealer orders disconnected from main operations
- Generic CRMs (Zoho, etc.) missing solar-specific workflows

## Project types supported

Residential, commercial, and ground-mounted solar installations across India.
